# Storefront image rules

The rules every product photo on demfirat.com follows. Add new rules here as
they are agreed.

## Shape

- **Every image is square (1:1).** Product cards, the product page gallery and
  thumbnails all show square boxes, so a square photo is shown whole.
- A photo that is not square is cropped to fill the box, around its centre.
  Fix such a photo by re-cropping or re-shooting it square, not by giving the
  page a different shape.

## Sizes

| Use | Where it shows | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Main | Product page gallery | 2000 × 2000 px |
| Collection | Product cards in collection and category grids | 1200 × 1200 px |
| Thumbnail | Gallery thumbnails, small previews | 600 × 600 px |
